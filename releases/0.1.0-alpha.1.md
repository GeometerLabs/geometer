# Geometer 0.1.0-alpha.1

The first public alpha. Free to download, no account, no telemetry.

## What you can do

- **Find where your agent breaks a rule.** Geometer reads your project, works out what
  your agent can do and which business rules apply, and runs it in containers with things
  going wrong on purpose: a lost reply, a request cut short, part of a call applied, a lost
  acknowledgement, a crash. Outside services are simulated.
- **Understand it.** Each problem reads consequence first, with the run played step by
  step and what the runs where the rule broke had in common.
- **Replay it, check your fix, keep the check.** The same run, exactly; your change
  checked against it; and a kept check that `geo ci` runs from then on.
- **Earn authority, action by action.** The trust file shows what each action of your
  agent may do now and what evidence would make it ready for more. A person grants a level
  (with a limit, an owner and a review date), Guard enforces it, and a breach steps it down.
- **Watch, then stop.** Guard sits in front of a tool and first only records what it would
  have stopped; approving and denying held calls happens in the Live room.
- **Share a certificate.** A signed, self-contained report of what was checked, what held,
  what broke and what was not checked, with a dated audit export.
- **See everything it does.** Under the hood shows Geometer's processes, containers, CPU
  in cores and the network activity it can see.

## Known limits

- **Not signed by Apple.** macOS asks you to allow the app once (see the README).
- **Apple silicon only on macOS**; no Intel Macs, no Windows, no Linux desktop app (on
  Linux, `geo open` opens the workspace in your browser).
- **Agents Geometer can run** are those whose tools it can find and simulate. When it
  cannot, it says what is missing instead of reporting a clean result.
- **Guard is for trying in development and staging.** It has not been qualified for
  production traffic.
- **Certificates are signed with a key made on your computer**, so they show integrity,
  not who issued them.
- Geometer has been used by its own team only. Your reports decide what we fix first.
